🏭 Manufacturing Department

Precision CNC, fabrication, and production — plus Research Division: Cosmetology, Telecommunications, Mealers, and Jewelleries.

Department Overview

Our manufacturing facility combines modern CNC technology with skilled fabrication and rigorous quality control to deliver parts and assemblies that meet exact specifications.

🖥️

CNC Machining

3-, 4-, and 5-axis CNC milling and turning for high-precision components in metals and engineering plastics.

🔥

Fabrication & Welding

Structural steel, stainless, and alloy fabrication with certified welding processes and NDT support.

🧩

Assembly & Integration

Mechanical and electro-mechanical assembly, testing, and packaging ready for site installation.

Quality Assurance

CMM inspection, material certificates, process control, and full traceability documentation.
